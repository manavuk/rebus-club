The 15-step climb

Five easy picture puzzles, then five medium word layouts, then five hard ones. Hints are always there if you're stuck, but they shave points. You have three lives; giving up on a puzzle costs one.

How high can you go?

Endless never runs out. Every five puzzles you solve, you go up a level and win back a life. Levels 1–3 use the built-in puzzles. From level 4 on you get Expert puzzles that combine two or three tricks, and when you’ve seen them all, Claude writes new ones as you climb. Every one it writes is kept for Practice too.

    How to read a rebus
    • Position MIND above MATTER is “mind over matter”.
    • Count Three CYCLEs make a tricycle.
    • Say it aloud B4 is “before”. CCCC is “foresees”.
    • Size and colour A huge DEAL is a big deal.
    • Direction and gaps Letters stepping down spell downtown. A gap means split, space or apart.
    • What’s missing An alphabet with no L is “Noel”.
    • Keyboard Enter checks your answer, then moves on. ? (or H outside the answer box) gives a hint. Esc skips in Practice.
